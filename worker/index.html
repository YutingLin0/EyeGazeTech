10-minute facilitation plan
0:00–2:30
Join with a nickname and spend this time practicing. Agree on webcam gaze or mouse dwell as a class before starting. Keep click and keyboard available for access needs.
2:30–5:30
Start the match and play three phrase rounds. Each correct message earns 100 points plus up to 30 for time left.
5:30–7:30
Review the leaderboard, then try the practice board with different tile sizes or dwell times.
7:30–10:00
Discuss accidental selections and how personalization could support daily communication.
Camera and accessibility

This classroom prototype uses a standard webcam to estimate gaze. The video is processed in your browser and the activity does not save camera frames or calibration data. Select either mouse mode to turn the camera off. Tab to a control and press Enter or Space to activate it.

The TD I-Series uses dedicated eye-tracking hardware. Classroom webcam accuracy varies with lighting, positioning, and the device. Try mouse dwell to explore the timing if webcam tracking is difficult.
